The Problem

Traditional social platforms do not care about what is being said.
They care about who is saying it.

Followers Profiles Likes Algorithms Outrage

Modern platforms optimize for persistent identity to capture attention, cultivate reputation anxiety, and maximize engagement loops.

Chorus intentionally rejects these concepts. There are no profiles to build, no follower counts to farm, and no algorithms deciding what you should think.

Core Principles

Designed for dialogue, not engagement.

Thread-Scoped Identity

Your temporary name exists only within the bounds of a single thread. You cannot be tracked across different discussions.

No Popularity Metrics

Zero upvotes, likes, or follower counts. Content is evaluated by what is expressed, not who expressed it.

Privacy by Design

IP addresses are evaluated at the edge for optional geolocation and are never written to disk.

Engineering

How Chorus works under the hood.

Native Git Append-Only Storage

Identity is the product experience. Underneath, Chorus persists every discussion thread and message as native Git commits—making the platform transparent, auditable, and open-source without proprietary vendor lock-in.
